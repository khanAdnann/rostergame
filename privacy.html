DATA NOTICE / RL-01

Privacy, plainly.

Roadster Loop is a free browser game. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no advertising scripts, and no server-side player profiles. This page explains the small amount of data the game touches and the third-party requests required to load it.

Information stored on your device

The game uses your browser's local storage to remember two preferences: your best score and whether sound is muted. This information stays in your browser, is not transmitted to us, and can be removed at any time by clearing site data for this page.

No analytics or advertising

We do not run analytics, tracking pixels, advertising networks, or cross-site profiling. The site does not set advertising cookies. An ads.txt file is published to state that no advertising systems are authorized.

Third-party resources

The game loads the following resources directly from third-party content delivery networks:

  • Three.js from cdnjs (Cloudflare) to render the 3D scene.
  • Space Grotesk and Syncopate fonts from Google Fonts.

When your browser requests these files, the relevant provider may receive standard request information such as your IP address, browser user agent, and referring page. That processing is governed by the provider's own privacy policy. No third-party resource is used to identify you or to build a profile of your play.

Audio

Sound effects are synthesized locally in your browser with the Web Audio API. The game does not record audio, request microphone access, or upload any audio data.

Children's privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because there are no accounts or data submissions, no personal information is sent to us through the game.

Changes

If this game later adds features that change how data is handled, this page will be updated before those features go live. The date below records the current version.
